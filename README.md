# Yavoth Demon Remix, remixed SV08 MAX mounts 
See STEP files for modified BTT Eddy Duo mount and modified toolhead mount, modified to allow mounting between the stock belt clamps on the stock Sovol mounting bracket.
**Major differences between this version and the Demon Remix:**
  - Mount fits directly to the carriage block through the stock Sovol toolhead mounting bracket that also retains the ends of the belts.
     - In order to achieve this you must remove the 4 short bolts holding the stock mounting bracket and install the modified Yavoth toolhead mount with longer bolts that go through both the modified mount and the stock toolhead mounting bracket to screw into the carriage block. 
## Build notes for SV08 MAX mount through stock mounting bracket. 
This new geometry changes assembly order, but fits together very nicely. 
Parts were printed in Sunlu EasyABS (mounts) and generic ASA (housing) with clear PETG for the LED holders. Parts were printed on my evil Bambu X1C, which I hate to love.
  - There is one bolt in the modified mount that goes the other way, screwing into the toolhead, and it must be installed before the modified mount can be bolted to the carriage.
     - This is because once the modified mount it installed, you still need to turn that bolt into the nut in the toolhead from the back and there’s just enough clearance over the stock mounting bracket to get a hex wrench in there, but not enough clearance to put the bolt in from the back. Learned this twice…
  - Eddy Duo must be mounted on the hotend side of the Eddy mount, with the Eddy Duo toolboard side away from the hotend. Tight cable work, but you gotta run the wiring back towards the hotend between the top of the Eddy Duo and the bottom of the mount, then straight up and over, otherwise it will get pinched between the modified mount and the stock toolhead mounting bracket. There’s just enough clearance.           - Also, I flashed Katapult with a boot loader offset to my Eddy Duo so that I could reflash over CAN if I ever need to, since it’s so tight in there and it’s a pain to get off if you have to flash it over USB. Try to mount the Eddy Duo between 2 and 3mm above the nozzle. Use Loctite. Learned this twice…
  - You will need some pretty long bolts to get all the way through the toolhead and modified mount to screw into the stock toolhead bracket, which is threaded.
     - M3, but I don’t remember exactly how long, maybe 30mm. Use Loctite, and don’t forget to thread the bolt that you placed in the back of the modified mount into the nut that's placed in the toolhead shroud!
  - Finally, and maybe most importantly, you will need shorter threaded standoffs than those that were included with the EBB36 (or was it the Orbiter? I can’t remember, so much hardware)
      - This remix puts the toolhead closer to the carriage block, and that means the toolhead board (mounted behind the extruder) hangs out far enough that the wiring will almost certainly collide with the big wiring harness/PTFE tube support bracket thingy bolted to the gantry of the SV08 MAX. I only had to learn this once, but I learned it the hard way. 
      - I didn’t have much of a selection of PCB standoffs in my stash so I ended up cutting a 20mm standoff down to about 13mm with a rotary tool and that gave me just enough clearance, but it definitely tightens the gap between the back of the extruder stepper and the toolhead board. I’ve been keeping an eye on the temps to make sure that smaller clearance doesn’t cause overheating issues. So far so good and I’ve probably put 100 or so hours on this new toolhead.
  - I also installed the O2 Orbiter Filament Sensor (designed by Robert Lorincz) since I’m not currently using the filament buffer (thanks Mainline) and would otherwise have no runout sensor. This filament sensor is worth every penny and I love it. Do a full calibration, including Eddy temperature drift calibration. Don’t skip input shaper!
## Some advantages of this setup for SV08 MAX:
** Nozzle is in exactly the same physical position as stock toolhead with regards to carriage block, so no need to change that offset. You will need to change the Eddy offset, which is something like X=0, Y=26.72
** X-endstop switch is in exactly the same physical position as stock toolhead, and will contact the end stop trigger tab on the gantry perfectly. I used the standard Omron [D2F-5?] held on with just nuts and bolts. 
** You don’t have to buy another set of longer belts to use this toolhead! Which means you also don’t have to go through the tedious process of tensioning them. You can swap the stock toolhead back in without having to mess with the belts at all, just make sure you keep the shorter bolts that went through the stock mounting bracket.
